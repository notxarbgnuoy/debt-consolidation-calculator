Assumptions

  • This calculator is a tool to help consumers compare potential scenarios. It does not quote or advertise rates. Inputs are the sole responsibility of the user; always double-check the information entered and the results.
  • Payments, balances and interest use your inputs, monthly amortization and annual interest rates divided by 12. Actual lender calculations may differ.
  • Consumer debts use the fixed monthly payments entered to estimate their remaining amortization (time to pay off). No new borrowing is modeled.
  • HELOC projections use the entered draw and repayment periods and a constant example rate. Real HELOC rates and payments can change.
  • Blended rates are balance-weighted nominal rates, not APRs or measures of total cost. Closing fees and mortgage insurance are separate from interest.
  • The current household budget includes escrow and mortgage insurance. These expenses are paid first and cannot be used as extra principal. Remaining funds and freed payments go to the highest-rate remaining debt. An insufficient budget stops that projection.
  • Existing PMI continues until that mortgage is paid off; early cancellation is not modeled. Conventional refinance assumes no PMI. FHA MIP uses scheduled annual premiums and its modeled duration, ending when the loan is paid off.
  • Tax and insurance estimates are editable assumptions, not local quotes. Recurring housing costs stay constant and continue after loan payoff.
  • Current keeps your actual debts and provides no additional cash. Financing options may include extra cash and financed fees, so compare starting balances as well as payments.
  • This tool has been rigorously tested, but users are responsible for checking its accuracy, especially before making any financial decisions.

For educational purposes only. Illustrations are not a loan offer, approval, or commitment to lend. Actual rates, payments, fees, and eligibility vary. No financial inputs are transmitted by this calculator.
