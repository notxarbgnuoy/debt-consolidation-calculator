Understanding Blended Rates & Consolidation Strategies

When you have multiple debts—such as an existing first mortgage, credit cards, auto loans, and personal loans—calculating your weighted average blended rate reveals the true cost of your overall debt portfolio. Comparing a cash-out refinance against a HELOC allows you to evaluate whether trading a low primary mortgage rate for a single larger loan is financially superior to taking a second lien and leaving your first mortgage untouched.

How Blended Rate is Calculated

Weighted Blended APR = Σ(Balance × APR) ÷ ΣBalance. This balances each interest rate by its proportion of your total debt, giving an accurate effective rate across all obligations.

Cash-Out Refinance vs. HELOC

A cash-out refinance replaces your current mortgage entirely with a new, larger loan at current market rates. A HELOC acts as a second mortgage, allowing you to preserve an existing 3% or 4% primary mortgage while consolidating high-APR cards.

The Power of Payoff Acceleration

Consolidation lowers your required monthly payment, immediately freeing up cash flow. If you maintain discipline and continue paying your previous total monthly budget towards principal, you can eliminate debt years early and save thousands in interest.
